FIRST NOTE

Why I’m keeping a record of the journey

A portfolio is more useful when it tells a story, not just a list of credentials.

Medical training is made up of thousands of small moments — patients, lessons, mistakes, conversations, long days, and the gradual confidence that comes with doing the work.

I wanted a place to keep track of that journey: the clinical experiences that shaped me, the academic work that challenged me, and the ideas I want to return to.

This journal will grow slowly. For now, this first note is simply a marker: the beginning of documenting what comes next.
